About Str Style

Two shopfronts, one street, one standard.

STR Style is the shared name behind two independent businesses run side by side in Polepettai East, Thoothukudi: STR Salon, a men's grooming studio, and STR Tailors, a ladies' tailoring house.

Each business keeps its own space, its own team and its own identity — but both are held to the same idea: work that's done properly, explained clearly, and priced fairly, every single visit.

If you're looking for a haircut or grooming session, head to Str Salon. If you need a blouse stitched, a dress altered, or something custom made, Str Tailors is next door.
