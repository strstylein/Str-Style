About Str Tailors

Tailoring, taken personally.

Str Tailors is based at 27/3 Polepettai East, Thoothukudi — a ladies' tailoring shop where measurements are taken properly and fittings are never an afterthought.

From everyday blouse stitching to bridal wear and fully custom dresses, every order goes through the same process: a careful measurement, an honest timeline, and a fitting you're happy with before it leaves the shop.
